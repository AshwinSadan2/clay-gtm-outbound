## Issues and notes

- **Permission checks:** applying migration 005 and two loads were blocked by the safety check until the owner explicitly approved; the blocks were respected, not worked around.
- **zsh word splitting** broke a `$F` flags string again in testing: every skill now says to use a bash array.
- **RapidAPI counts every call:** a 404 test and the `/stats` usage check each cost a call (2 of 100).
- **Cloudflare bot block** (`403 error code: 1010`) on Python's default User-Agent: fixed with a browser-style one.
- **Debounce credits:** 100 checks used 98 credits; the 2 `Unknown` results appear to be free (unconfirmed).
- **Two Icypeas `ultra_sure` emails bounced** at Debounce: the finder's own confidence isn't enough.
- **Thin scans:** within3.com blocked the scanner (403 → 3 tools only); landing.ai, close.com, coderabbit.ai returned 3–4 tools.
- **Sequencers never show** on public websites (0 of 95), as the universe doc predicts.
