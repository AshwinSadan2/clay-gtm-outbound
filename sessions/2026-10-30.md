## What we learned
- On Luna, log-probabilities (confidence) and temperature only work with effort `none`; anything else is an HTTP 400.
- Curly apostrophes broke phrase matching until normalised.
- v2 prompt changes (current work, one thing, B ≤ 15 words) cut openers from 16 to 11 words and raised confidence 0.90 → 0.96.
- The insight prompt never admits "no clear link", so the review flag barely fires.
- `--max-cost` can overshoot by up to one batch of running rows.
